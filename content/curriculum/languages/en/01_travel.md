# Airport & Flight Essentials

## 1. At the Check-in Counter & Baggage Drop

When arriving at the international airport terminal, the first step is approaching the airline check-in desk. Key terms include *boarding pass*, *carry-on luggage* (hand luggage), and *checked baggage*. Common phrases include *"May I see your passport and booking reference?"* and *"Did you pack these bags yourself?"*.

> [!NOTE] 🤯 Dato WOW
> In international aviation English, **"carry-on luggage"** is American English, whereas **"hand luggage"** or **"cabin bag"** is standard in British English.

## 2. Security Check & 3D CT Scanners

At security control, you must place your electronic devices and metallic items in separate trays before passing through the metal detector. Key vocabulary includes *conveyor belt*, *tray*, *boarding gate*, and *customs declaration*.

> [!NOTE] 🤯 Dato WOW
> Next-generation 3D computed tomography (CT) scanners in 2026 allow passengers to keep laptops and liquids inside their bags during inspection.

---

## 3. Boarding Gate, In-Flight Service & Customs

At the gate, listen for boarding groups (*"We are now inviting passengers in Zone 1 to board"*). Onboard, the flight attendants will assist with overhead bins, tray tables, and in-flight meals (*"Would you prefer chicken or pasta?"*).

> [!TIP] 🚀 Actualidad Científica 2026
> In 2026, major international airports employ biometric facial scanning (*Biometric Boarding Gates*) that verify your passport and ticket in under 2 seconds.

> [!CASE] Dialogue in Context
> - *Agent:* "Good morning, where are you flying to today?"
> - *Traveler:* "I'm flying to London Heathrow on flight BA458."
> - *Agent:* "Are you checking any bags or do you only have carry-on luggage?"
> - *Traveler:* "Just this suitcase to check in, and a backpack for the cabin."
